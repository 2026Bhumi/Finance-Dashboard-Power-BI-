## Finance Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive finance dashboard built using Power BI to analyze sales, profitability, performance across countries, products, and time periods. The dashboard combines KPI tracking, trend analysis, and interactive filtering to support data-driven decision-making.

---

## Objective

To design a multi-page financial dashboard with intuitive navigation, enabling users to:

* Monitor key financial metrics
* Analyze profit trends and performance drivers
* Explore data dynamically using filters and drilldowns

---

## Tools & Technologies

* Power BI
* DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
* Power Query

---

## Dataset

The dataset includes financial and sales-related fields such as:

* **Sales, Profit, Gross Sales**
* **COGS (Cost of Goods Sold), Manufacturing Price**
* **Discounts, Discount Band**
* **Units Sold, Sale Price**
* **Country, Product, Segment**
* **Date, Month, Year**

---

## Dashboard Features

### Navigation

* Multi-page navigation (Page 1–Page 8) for structured data exploration
* Dedicated **region-specific view (West Region Detail)**

---

### KPI Cards

* **Total Sales:** 19.61M
* **Total Profit:** -614.55K
* **Profit Margin:** 0.12

These KPIs provide an instant overview of overall financial performance.

---

### Visualizations

#### Trend Analysis

* Line chart showing **Sales vs Profit over months**
* Reveals fluctuations and seasonal trends

#### Profit by Country

* Donut chart comparing profitability across:

  * France
  * Germany
  * Mexico
  * Canada
  * United States

#### Profit by Product

* Bar chart highlighting product-level profitability
* Identifies **loss-making vs high-performing products**

---

### Slicers (Filters)

* Segment filter (e.g., Enterprise)
* Enables dynamic dashboard interaction and focused analysis

---

### External Link Integration

* “**Visit Finance Portal**” button for quick access to external resources/websites

---

## Key Insights

* Despite strong **total sales (19.61M)**, overall profit is **negative**, indicating high costs or discount impact
* Certain products (e.g., Carretera) contribute significantly to losses
* Profitability varies across countries, with some regions underperforming
* Sales show upward trends in later months, but profit does not increase proportionally

---

## Business Recommendations

* Review high-cost products and optimize pricing or production costs
* Re-evaluate discount strategies to improve profit margins
* Focus on high-performing regions and products for revenue growth
* Investigate cost drivers causing negative profitability
